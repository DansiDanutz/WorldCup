# YouTube Live Run Of Show

Target runtime: 18 to 22 minutes.

Primary goal: make viewers understand the app fast, feel the collecting loop, and sign up.

## Before Going Live

- Open `https://worldcup26.world/`.
- Open `https://worldcup26.world/predictions#legend-cards`.
- Keep the `Promote/cards/sheets/` folder ready for quick visual cutaways.
- Keep the YouTube channel open in another tab.
- Test audio before starting.
- Keep the sign-up link pinned in chat: `https://worldcup26.world/`.

## Live Structure

### 0:00-1:00 - Hook

Show the homepage.

Explain that this is not just another football app. It is a prediction game, a story library, and a card collection built around football history.

CTA: "Stay with me for a few minutes and I will show you how to start collecting."

### 1:00-3:00 - The Simple Game

Show the pick-3-teams idea.

Explain:

- Everyone can join free.
- You pick 3 nations.
- Your leaderboard score only starts from your sign-up and pick date.
- The leaderboard is for fun, bragging rights, and football community.

### 3:00-6:00 - The Collection Loop

Show the Legend Card Collection top section.

Explain the loop:

1. Read the card preview.
2. Listen to the story in the app.
3. Open the matching YouTube story.
4. Come back and collect the card.

This is the main product loop.

### 6:00-9:00 - Card Categories

Show each tab:

- Did You Know cards.
- Series episode cards.
- Supporter wallpaper cards.
- Bonus legend cards.

Explain that every card must be unique. A card belongs to one story, one video, one reason to unlock it.

### 9:00-11:00 - Why YouTube Matters

Open the YouTube channel.

Explain that videos are not separate from the app. The app and the channel work together:

- You watch a football story.
- The story gives meaning to the card.
- The card gives the viewer a reason to return.
- New uploads mean new cards.

### 11:00-13:00 - Why People Should Sign Up Now

Show the app again.

Explain that the collection is early, so early users can start building their album before it becomes huge.

Strong CTA:

> Sign up now, pick your 3 teams, and start with the cards already live.

### 13:00-15:30 - Future Vision

Show card sheets and supporter cards.

Explain the future structure:

- World Cup now.
- Euros next.
- Copa America.
- Champions League.
- Europa League.
- Conference League.
- African Cup of Nations.

The long-term promise: football stories become collectible artifacts.

### 15:30-18:30 - Structure Recap And Live Chat

Show the collection tabs again.

Explain the three engines: game, story, collection.

Ask live chat:

- Which 3 teams would you pick?
- Which nation do you support?
- Which card should be next?

### 18:30-21:30 - Close

Return to the homepage.

Repeat the app link and the first action:

> Go to WorldCup26.world. Sign up. Pick 3 teams. Open the Legends tab. Start collecting.

End by inviting comments:

> Tell me in chat which nation you picked and which card you want next.
