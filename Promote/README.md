# WorldCup26 Promote Pack

This folder is a ready-to-use promotion kit for WorldCup26.world.

Use it for YouTube Live, YouTube videos, Shorts, X, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, Reddit, partner outreach, and paid ad creative.

## What Is Inside

- `screenshots/` - live app screenshots from desktop and mobile.
- `cards/did-you-know/` - polished Did You Know card images from the YouTube shorts library.
- `cards/supporters/` - all 48 nation supporter portrait cards.
- `cards/legendary-production/` - real production legend-card assets pulled from the live app manifest.
- `cards/sheets/` - contact sheets for quick video editing and visual selection.
- `scripts/youtube-live-15-minute-script.md` - long-form YouTube Live host script.
- `scripts/live-run-of-show.md` - practical live show structure.
- `scripts/social-copy-bank.md` - reusable short captions and calls to action.

## Core Message

WorldCup26.world is a free football prediction and card-collection app.

Users pick 3 teams, follow the leaderboard, watch WorldCup26 Legends stories on YouTube, listen to card stories in the app, and unlock collectible digital cards. The same structure can later expand beyond the World Cup into the Euros, Copa America, Champions League, Europa League, Conference League, and African Cup of Nations.

## Main Conversion Goal

Get the viewer to sign up and start collecting.

The strongest message is:

> Pick your 3 teams. Follow the leaderboard. Watch the stories. Unlock the cards. Build your football collection.

## Best Visual Order For A Live Demo

1. Open the homepage.
2. Show the sign-up / pick 3 teams concept.
3. Show the Legend Card Collection.
4. Show the card journey: read, listen, open YouTube, collect.
5. Show Did You Know cards.
6. Show episode cards.
7. Show supporter wallpaper cards.
8. Show bonus cards.
9. Show the match schedule and leaderboard.
10. Explain future tournaments and why the collection grows over time.

## Recommended CTA

Use this CTA often during the live:

> Go to WorldCup26.world, sign up, pick your 3 teams, and start collecting your first Legend cards today.
