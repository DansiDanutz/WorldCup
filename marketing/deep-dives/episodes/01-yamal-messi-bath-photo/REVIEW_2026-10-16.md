## Editorial verdict

The episode now tells a story: the 2026 final establishes the stakes, the 2007
raffle and bath photograph provide the mystery, Celia / 304 mirror the players'
origins, and the final returns to the opening before the card / product close.
This is materially more coherent than a fact list or code-only cut. However,
clip coverage alone does not establish spectacle. Much of the moving material
is stadiums and crowd reactions; the Getafe run and decisive final goal still
use drawn reconstructions, and the central photograph is an original drawn
reconstruction rather than an approved animated scene of both protagonists.
Do not describe this as a fully visually approved football film yet.
