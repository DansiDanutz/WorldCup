## The true-history hook (verified)
Two cursed crowns. **Egypt** were the first African and Arab nation to play a World Cup
(Italy **1934**) and have won the Africa Cup of Nations a **record 7 times** — yet across
three World Cups (1934, 1990, 2018) they have **never won a single match**. **Belgium** held
the FIFA world **No.1 ranking for nearly four years** and reached the 2018 semi-final, but
their golden generation never won a major trophy. 2026 is, almost certainly, their last dance.
