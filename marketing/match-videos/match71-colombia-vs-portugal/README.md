## Nation-unique mystic story (Rule #21) — RESEARCHED & SOURCED
**Colombia — El Dorado / the Muisca gilded king (Legend 071 "The Gilded King"):**
- The Muisca were master goldsmiths of the Andean Altiplano; they cast votive *tunjos*
  in gold/tumbaga as **offerings to the gods in sacred lakes — not wealth-hoarding**
  (gold as prayer, not greed). — en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muisca · /wiki/Muisca_art
- "El Dorado" ("the gilded one") was the investiture rite at **Lake Guatavita**: a new
  *zipa* coated in gold dust rafted out and threw gold + emeralds into the water
  (chronicle: Rodríguez Freyle's *El Carnero*, 1636 — narrate as **legend**). —
  en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Dorado
- Conquistadors twisted the gilded *man* into a *city of gold*; **they drained Lake
  Guatavita to the mud** (Sepúlveda ~1580; a British firm 1909–11) and found almost
  nothing. — liquisearch.com/lake_guatavita/historical_attempts_to_recover_the_gold
- The golden **Muisca Raft** (balsa muisca, c.1295–1410, found 1969 near Pasca) is the
  centrepiece of the **Museo del Oro, Bogotá**. — en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muisca_raft

**Portugal — Sebastianism / O Encoberto (cold-open motif):**
- King **Sebastião I** ("O Desejado") died at 24 at the **Battle of Alcácer-Quibir
  (1578)**; his body was never reliably identified → no heir → the **Iberian Union
  (1580)**. — britannica.com/biography/Sebastian-king-of-Portugal · en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Alcácer_Quibir
- **Sebastianism**: the messianic belief he did not die and would return as **"O
  Encoberto" (the Hidden One)** to save Portugal. The "ride back out of the fog" detail
  is popular legend (not in Wikipedia/Britannica) — narrated **as legend**. —
  en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastianism

**The honest bridge:** there is **no** direct Portuguese–El Dorado link (it was Spanish:
Quesada, Pizarro). The script never claims one — it frames *two nations chasing
something golden and unreachable* (Colombia guarded gold it gave the gods; Portugal
sailed off the edge of the map / waits for a vanished king), decided tonight by
imagination, not force.
