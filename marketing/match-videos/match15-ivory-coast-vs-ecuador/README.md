## The true-history hook (verified)
On **8 October 2005**, the day Ivory Coast qualified for their first World Cup, captain
**Didier Drogba** and the whole squad knelt on live TV begging the warring sides to *"lay
down your weapons"* — it helped move the country toward a ceasefire. In **June 2007** Drogba
had an AFCON qualifier moved to the rebel stronghold **Bouaké**, where troops from both sides
watched together; Ivory Coast won 5–0 (*"five goals erase five years of war"*).
Secondary hook: Ivory Coast and Ecuador have **never met before** — their first game ever.
