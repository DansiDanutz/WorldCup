## The true-history hook (verified)
**Cape Verde** — ten volcanic islands, ~**525,000 people**, ~350 miles off West Africa — reached
their **first-ever World Cup** in 2026. For decades the islands were a nursery for bigger nations:
**Henrik Larsson** (Cape Verdean father) starred for Sweden, **Nani** (Cape Verdean descent)
won trophies with Portugal, **Gelson Martins** (born on the islands) plays for Portugal — the
islands gave the game its stars and kept none of them, until now.
Spain arrive as **2010 World Cup** and **Euro 2024** champions, ~48M strong, led by 18-year-old
**Lamine Yamal** (famously photographed as a baby being bathed by Messi in a 2007 charity shoot)
and **Rodri** (2024 Ballon d'Or — the first defensive midfielder of the modern era to win it).
