**Jordan — Legend 074 = "Aretas, Guardian of the Rose-Red Treasury":**

  allowed extensive settlement of an essentially arid area." — whc.unesco.org/en/list/326/

  (scholarly hypothesis → narrated as legend, not fact). — en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petra

**Argentina — cold-open motif (NOT the Legend): the Sol de Mayo + tango.**

  the popular "Inca sun / Inti" origin is later myth, not contemporary fact — used as motif. —
  en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_of_May
- "Albiceleste" = light-blue-and-white (sky and clouds). — en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Argentina
