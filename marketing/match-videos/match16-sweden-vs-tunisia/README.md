## The true-history hook (verified)
On **2 June 1978**, in Rosario, **Tunisia beat Mexico 3–1** to become the **first African
nation ever to win a match at a World Cup** — a barrier broken for the whole continent.
They kept knocking: at the **2022 World Cup, Tunisia beat France 1–0** (Education City,
30 Nov 2022). Yet across all of those tournaments they have **never once escaped the group
stage** — the door they opened, they've never walked through.
Sweden's modern edge is its strike pair: **Viktor Gyökeres** (couldn't hold a Premier League
place at Brighton, rebuilt himself via Coventry, then a ~38-goal season at Sporting CP earned
a big-money move to Arsenal) and **Alexander Isak** (the elegance to Gyökeres's power).
