# THE LEGENDS' GAME — Round One  (STORY BIBLE v2 · the 10/10 build)
### Messi × Mbappé × Haaland × Ronaldo — a mystery-thriller where a board game decides the World Cup

---

## 1. LOGLINE
Four football kings are summoned by a faceless Keeper to a glowing board where the
**World Cup itself** is the prize. They don't roll dice — their **real World Cup
performances** move them. After round one, the greatest of them all is in a cell…
and no one knows who is really dealing the game.

## 2. THEME
Greatness is not given, it's *earned every round.* The board is merciless and fair —
it rewards what you do, not who you are. (That's why Ronaldo's fall lands so hard.)

## 3. THE MYSTERY (the spine — set up early, paid off as a cliffhanger)
- **WHO is the Keeper?** A hooded golden figure who deals the game and never speaks.
  Hinted in the cold open, his face never shown; the final line asks *"Who is dealing
  this game… and what does he want?"* → Round Two promise. (Rewatch bait: viewers
  scan earlier shots for clues.)
- **WHY did Ronaldo fail?** Framed as eerie, almost rigged — "the board does not care
  about your name." Leaves doubt: bad round, or is the Keeper against him?

## 4. THE STAKES (raised from v1)
Not points — **the World Cup**. "Whoever rules this board lifts it. The rest go home
with nothing." Every move now carries finals-level weight.

## 5. THE RULES (revealed cinematically by Brian, as law, not instructions)
1. **Score a goal → you OWN what you touch** (buy property).
2. **Create a goal → you MOVE** (assist = advance).
3. **Do nothing → the board LOCKS YOU AWAY** (no goal/assist = jail).
> Shown as three glowing rune-cards burning their icons into the air. No rulebook text.

## 6. TONE & STYLE
Pixar-grade 3D, but graded like a **cinematic thriller**: deep shadow, volumetric
god-rays, cold-blue vs warm-gold contrast, film grain, slow push-ins, one speed-ramp.
Brian (real ElevenLabs) narrates low and deliberate. SOCCER ONLY. No on-screen
sentences. Real results only.

## 7. CHARACTERS & ARCS (recognizable likenesses — Kling-animated from photo anchors)
- **Messi 🇦🇷 — The Quiet King.** Hat-trick vs Algeria. Arc: serene, total control.
  "He did not celebrate. He simply collected." Owns the richest corner. *Leads.*
- **Mbappé 🇫🇷 — The Sprinter.** Brace vs Senegal + the record card (58, France's best
  ever). Arc: blazing, hungry, climbing fast.
- **Haaland 🇳🇴 — The Builder.** Brace vs Iraq. Arc: cold, methodical; stacks a wall in
  the north. Relentless.
- **Ronaldo 🇵🇹 — The Fallen King (the drama core).** 0 goals, 1–1 vs DR Congo. Arc:

  *waiting.* "They counted him out before. The world remembers how that ended."
  His cornered defiance is the emotional climax and the Round-Two hook.
- **The Keeper — The Mystery.** Dealer of the game, owner of the trophy, identity hidden.
