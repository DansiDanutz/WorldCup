### A WorldCup26 Special · Messi × Mbappé × Haaland × Ronaldo

## The premise (one line)

Four kings of football don't settle the World Cup on the pitch tonight — they
settle it on a **giant glowing Monopoly board** shaped like the road to the
trophy. And here's the twist: **they don't roll dice. Their REAL World Cup
performances roll for them.** What they did in the first round of WC2026 has
already decided who advances, who buys, and who sits in jail.

This is a *match between them* — a board-game duel — but every move is earned on
grass, not luck.

---

## Why this is true to life (the real first round of WC2026)

The board is powered by what actually happened in **Round 1 of the 2026 World Cup
group stage** (sources in `README.md`):

| Legend | Nation | Round 1 result | On the board |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lionel Messi** | Argentina | **Hat-trick** in a **3–0** win vs Algeria | 3 goals → **buys 3 properties**, owns a full colour set → **board leader** |
| **Kylian Mbappé** | France | **Brace** in a **3–1** win vs Senegal; becomes France's all-time top scorer (58) | 2 goals → **buys 2 properties** + draws a golden "Record" Chance card |
| **Erling Haaland** | Norway | **Brace** in a **4–1** win vs Iraq | 2 goals → **buys 2 properties**, builds the "Viking Wall" |
| **Cristiano Ronaldo** | Portugal | **No goal, no assist** in a **1–1** draw vs DR Congo | 0 goals, 0 assists → **STAYS IN JAIL** |

Nothing is invented: Ronaldo really did go scoreless in a 1–1 draw, which is
exactly why, under the game's own rules, **he is the one behind bars** — plotting
his escape for Round Two.

## The world

A cathedral-sized hall at night. No pitch, no crowd roar — just one colossal
**Monopoly board glowing like a runway to the World Cup trophy**, its squares
lit as famous football cities. Four ornate thrones at the four corners. Above it
all floats the **Mystery Banker** — a hooded, golden, faceless figure (the
"keeper of the game") who deals the cards and never speaks. (He is our recurring
mystery hook; his identity is teased, never revealed.)

The aesthetic is the channel's signature **Pixar-style 3D**: rounded, warm,
cinematic, soccer-only (round-neck shirts, a stadium skyline in the windows, NO
helmets, NO American football).

## Story beats (the 5-minute arc)

### 1 — COLD OPEN / THE HOOK (0:00–0:22)
Darkness. A single golden coin spins and lands. The board ignites city by city.
The Mystery Banker raises a hand and four thrones light up. *Narrator:* "Four
kings. One board. The first round is over — and the dice have already been
cast." The hook question: who is winning the game they never agreed to play?

### 2 — THE RULES (0:22–0:50)
The Banker deals three cards that float and burn their icons into the air —
shown as **pure icon animations, no rulebook text**:
- ⚽ **A goal buys a property.**
- 🅰️ **An assist moves your token.**
- ⛓️ **Do nothing, and you stay in jail.**
The narrator lays the rules out loud. The tension: everyone already knows where
this leaves one of them.

### 3 — MESSI, THE LANDLORD (0:50–1:25)
Argentina's hat-trick replays as three golden footballs drop into Messi's hands.
He calmly places **three houses** on a sky-blue-and-white avenue, lights flooding
his corner. He doesn't celebrate — he just owns it. *He is top of the board.*

### 4 — MBAPPÉ, THE RECORD (1:25–2:00)
Mbappé blurs around the board — fastest token in the game. Two strikes vs Senegal
become **two properties**, and the Banker deals him a **glowing golden Chance
card**: *France's all-time top scorer.* He flips it, grins, and surges up the
table behind Messi.

### 5 — HAALAND, THE WALL (2:00–2:35)
Haaland slams **two properties** down like he's laying concrete, then stacks them
into a **"Viking Wall."** Norway's debutant power, brick by brick. Cold, relentless,
climbing.

### 6 — RONALDO IN JAIL (2:35–3:25) — *the emotional core*
The camera finds the one dark corner. **Ronaldo, behind glowing bars.** A 1–1
draw, no goal, no assist — the rules are merciless. He grips the bars (slow-mo,
heartbeat under it), stares at the board he can't move on. He rolls for doubles —
the dice tumble — and they fail. The crowd outside murmurs. But his jaw sets.
This isn't the end of his story. **It's the setup.**

### 7 — THE STANDINGS (3:25–4:10)
A wide shot of the whole board: **Messi's blue empire leads**, Mbappé and Haaland's
corners glowing in pursuit, Ronaldo's corner dark behind bars. The Banker tallies
the round. The leaderboard burns into the air as **tokens and stacks, not text.**

### 8 — ROUND TWO IS LOADED (4:10–4:45)
The Banker sweeps the cards back into the deck. The board resets to neutral light.
The dice rise into the air and hang, spinning. *Narrator:* "Round one built the
empires. Round two can burn them down — and the man in jail has the most to prove."
Ronaldo's eyes flick up. The bars flicker.

### 9 — CTA / OUTRO (4:45–5:10)
Pull back to the whole glowing hall and the WC2026 trophy above the board.
*Narrator:* "You don't have to watch the kings play. Play their game. Pick your
three teams at worldcup26.world — free to play, just for fun, no prizes. Climb the
board. The next round kicks off now." Subscribe sting. Mystery Banker's hood turns
to camera — and the screen cuts to black on the spinning coin.
