# THE LEGENDS' GAME — SEASON MYSTERY ARC (to the World Cup Final)
### How the mystery stays "activated" every episode, from the group stage to July 19

This is the spine that keeps viewers hooked across the whole saga. Each video maps to a
real WC2026 stage, escalates the mystery, pays off a prior question, and ends on a
cliffhanger. Numbers/scorers/winners are filled in **only once each real stage is played.**

---

## REAL WC2026 STRUCTURE (the calendar we ride)
- **Group stage** → top 2 of 12 groups + 8 best 3rd-placed = **32 advance**.
- **Round of 32** — NEW (first ever), **Jun 28 – Jul 3**.
- **Round of 16**, **Quarter-finals**, **Semi-finals**.
- **FINAL — Sunday, July 19, 2026, MetLife Stadium (East Rutherford, NJ), 3pm ET.**
- Knockouts: single-elimination; level after 90' → 30' extra time → penalties.
*(Sources at bottom.)*

## THE CENTRAL MYSTERY (one question, peeled in layers)
**Who really controls the board — and what do they want?** Revealed one skin at a time so
there is always a live question on the table:
1. *Who is the Keeper?* → **Pelé, the King.** (paid off R2)
2. *Why did the King free Ronaldo / why does Brazil never roll bad?* → **the three witches.** (R2)
3. *Who truly runs the board?* → **the witches, who feed on goals.** (group finale)
4. *What is the witches' price / endgame?* → **they feed on the FALLEN, and mean to devour
   the champion** (knockouts).
5. *Can anyone beat them?* → **only a king the FANS choose** (semis → final).

## THE "ACTIVATION" FORMULA (every episode does all four)
- **OPEN** a new question in the cold open (a fresh clue/threat).
- **DROP A CLUE** that rewards re-watching (the recurring motifs below).
- **PAY OFF** one earlier question (a real reveal — never stall).
- **CLIFFHANGER** the last 20s → the next stage + a prediction prompt.

**Recurring motifs (the breadcrumb trail):**
- **The spinning golden coin** in every cold open = the witches' *eye* (reveal late that it
  has watched the whole time).
- **The empty throne** that never gets filled = waiting for the *true* king (filled only at
  the Final).
- **The Golden Boot** = the witches' lure (the prize that's secretly a trap).
- **The three witches' count** — each stage they grow brighter (visually track their power).

## SEASON MAP — mystery beat per stage (fill ▢ after the real games)

### ▣ GROUP STAGE (R1–R3) — DONE / in progress
Keeper→Pelé→witches revealed. Ronaldo caged then freed (+2). Kane jailed. Brazil's heir
(Neymar/Vinícius — viewer poll) enters. **Output:** Golden Boot leaderboard + bracket set.

### ▣ GROUP-STAGE FINALE (the ~10-min Golden Boot video) — assets prepared, awaits R3 numbers
The board burns to reveal the true law (most goals wins); witches feed; King loses control;
contenders finish level; board folds into the **knockout bracket** and **freezes**.
**Cliffhanger:** the bracket can't crown a champion — the knockouts haven't been played.
*Fill after R3:* the leaderboard goals, which Brazilian entered, who's top of the boot.

### ▢ ROUND OF 32 — "THE CULL" (Jun 28–Jul 3)
**Mystery beat:** the witches begin **claiming the eliminated** — losers are dragged into the
dark of the board, gone forever. **Reveal:** the board doesn't just feed on goals — it feeds
on the **fallen.** **New question:** is the King quietly *shielding* one player from the cull?
**Cliffhanger:** 32 → 16; one big name is pulled under; "who did the King save, and why?"
*Fill after R32:* who advanced/eliminated, the shock exits, the boot leaders.

### ▢ ROUND OF 16 — "THE PRICE"
**Mystery beat:** the witches name their **price** — each round, one legend must be *spent*
to keep the game alive, and the **King must choose.** **Reveal:** the King's bargain to bring
Brazil's heir onto the board had a hidden cost he's been paying in secret. **Cliffhanger:**
the King hesitates over who to sacrifice; "what did the King already trade away?"
*Fill after R16:* the 8 survivors, the heartbreak exit, boot race.

### ▢ QUARTER-FINALS — "THE BETRAYAL"
**Mystery beat:** the heir (Neymar/Vinícius) discovers he's been **fattened for sacrifice** —
the witches plan to crown a champion only to **devour** him; the Golden Boot is a trap.
One witch **turns.** **Reveal:** the cup itself is the witches' cage. **Cliffhanger:** the
legends realise winning could doom them; "is the trophy the prize — or the bait?"
*Fill after QF:* the final 4, the boot top-2.

### ▢ SEMI-FINALS — "THE UPRISING"
**Mystery beat:** the legends unite (rebellion 2.0) **with** the King against the witches;
the King makes a **sacrifice/stand.** **Reveal:** the witches **cannot touch a champion
chosen by the people** — the empty throne belongs to whoever the *fans* crown. **Cliffhanger:**
two legends left, one Golden Boot, the witches at full power; "the fans decide the king."
*Fill after SF:* the 2 finalists, the boot decider.

### ▢ THE FINAL — "THE KING IS CROWNED" (Jul 19, MetLife)
**Payoff:** the **real** Golden Boot + the **real** World Cup winner decide the board's king.
The witches are beaten not by the King but by the **fans' choice**; the **empty throne is
filled.** **Final sting:** the golden coin spins once more — the board never truly ends (the
witches' wish), a door left open for next time. **CTA:** worldcup26.world — you crowned him.
*Fill after the Final:* the champion, the Golden Boot winner, the score.
