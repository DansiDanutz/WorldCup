# 🎲 THE LEGENDS' MONOPOLY CUP — Official Game Rules
### "It's a match between them — but the pitch decides every move."

A duel for four kings: **Messi · Mbappé · Haaland · Ronaldo.** The board is the
road to the World Cup. There is no luck here — **the players' REAL World Cup
performances are the dice.**

---

## 🟢 THE GOLDEN RULE
> **You don't roll dice. Your football does.**
> Everything you do on the real pitch in a World Cup round becomes your move on
> the board for that round.

---

## ⚽ THE THREE CORE RULES (these are the ones shown on screen)

### 1. A GOAL BUYS A PROPERTY 🏠
Every goal you score in the round lets you **buy one property** and place a house
on it. Score a hat-trick, buy three. Build a colour set and your rent (your lead)
multiplies.

### 2. AN ASSIST MOVES YOUR TOKEN 👟
Every assist (a pass that becomes a goal) **advances your token** forward around
the board — past Chance, past your rivals, closer to the trophy corner.

### 3. DO NOTHING → STAY IN JAIL ⛓️
No goal **and** no assist in the round? You are **sent to / kept in jail**. You
can't buy and you can't move. You sit out the round and try to break free next
time. *(This is exactly where Round 1 left Cristiano Ronaldo: 0 goals, 0 assists
in a 1–1 draw → behind bars.)*

---

## 🎟️ SPECIAL CARDS (the spice)

| Card | How you earn it | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 🟡 **RECORD Chance** | Break a real record in the round (e.g. become your nation's all-time top scorer) | Jump ahead extra squares + a free property *(Mbappé earned this in Round 1)* |
| 🔵 **HAT-TRICK Bonus** | Score 3+ in a single match | Instantly complete a colour set → board leader *(Messi earned this)* |
| 🧱 **WALL Bonus** | Score a brace in a debut-power run | Stack two houses into a fortified "Wall" *(Haaland earned this)* |
| 🗝️ **JAILBREAK** | Score in the NEXT round after being jailed | Break out, collect your missed turn, re-enter the game |
| 🟨 **CLEAN-SHEET Toll** | Your team concedes 0 | Charge rent (pressure) on the player who landed nearest |

> No card pays cash or prizes. Properties, houses and position are **points only**
> — this is **free to play, just for fun, no money, no prizes.**

---

## 🧮 HOW YOU WIN
Across the World Cup rounds, the legend who **owns the most of the board**
(properties + houses + position toward the trophy corner) is the **King of the
Board.** Jail can wipe out a round — but a JAILBREAK can flip the whole game. The
title is only decided when the real World Cup is.

## 📜 RULE NOTES (kept faithful to the channel)

  We never invent a scoreline.
