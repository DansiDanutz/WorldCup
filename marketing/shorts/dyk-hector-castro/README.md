## THE SUBJECT (verified, source-cited — hard rule #9)

**Héctor Castro — "El Divino Manco"** ("the Divine One-Armed Man"). Uruguay.
Born 29 Nov 1904, Montevideo · died 15 Sep 1960.

- **Age 13:** accidentally **amputated his right forearm with an electric saw** — hence
  *"El Manco"* ("the one-armed"). *"El Divino"* was added for how beautiful he was to watch.
- **1930 FIFA World Cup (the first ever):** scored **Uruguay's first-ever World Cup goal**
  (vs Peru, 18 Jul 1930, 1–0, first goal ever at the Estadio Centenario).
- **The final (30 Jul 1930):** scored the **89th-minute goal — the LAST goal of the match** —
  in Uruguay **4–2 Argentina**. The world's first champions were crowned by a one-armed man.
  He is the only man to score in a World Cup final with a single hand.
- Career: **1928 Olympic gold**, Copa América 1926 & 1935, 25 caps / 18 goals, 248 club
  goals for Nacional; later won five Uruguayan titles as Nacional's manager.

**Why it WOWs / why it's obscure:** almost nobody knows the very first World Cup was won —
and its final decided — by a centre-forward with one arm.
