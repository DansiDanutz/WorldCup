## The target (decided by our own data)

The blocker is **watch hours, not Shorts views.** Primary goal: **3,000 valid
public watch hours / 365 days** (fan‑funding) → **4,000** (full ads). Shorts are
the **discovery engine that feeds long‑form watch time**, not the monetization
path. ~3,000 hours ≈ **165 full‑equivalent views/day** — controllable. The 10M
Shorts/90d path (~33k views/day) is upside only.
