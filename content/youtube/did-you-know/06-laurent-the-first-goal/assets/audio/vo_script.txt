Did you know the man who scored the very first goal in World Cup history… went straight back to work in a car factory? 1930. The very first World Cup, in Uruguay. A young Frenchman named Lucien Laurent met a cross on the volley — and scored the first goal the tournament had ever seen. But Laurent was no star. He built cars for Peugeot, and had to take unpaid leave just to sail across the ocean and play. Then war came. Captured and held prisoner for three long years, he came home to find the soldiers had stolen his precious World Cup shirt. But Lucien had the last laugh. He lived to ninety-seven years old — long enough to watch France finally lift the World Cup, in 1998.
