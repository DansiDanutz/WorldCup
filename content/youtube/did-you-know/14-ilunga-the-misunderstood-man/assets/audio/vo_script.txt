Did you know the most ridiculed moment in World Cup history was actually an act of rebellion? 1974 — Zaire, against mighty Brazil. As Brazil lined up a free-kick, a defender named Mwepu Ilunga burst from the wall and booted the ball away. The world howled with laughter, and called it the dumbest moment football had ever seen. But Mwepu was not confused. His team had just learned the bonus they were promised was stolen by men close to their country's ruler. Unpaid, threatened, far from home, he tried to get himself sent off — the only protest he had left.
