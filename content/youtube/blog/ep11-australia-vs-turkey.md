## The secret nobody talks about

Australia are the island continent, football's great survivors — the team that in 2022 pushed Argentina to the very brink. Their captain and goalkeeper, Mathew Ryan, has played in six leagues across four continents, and this is his fourth World Cup; home, for him, is wherever he fights. Turkey are wanderers too, the bridge between East and West, and in 2002 they finished third at a World Cup, a golden age the country has been chasing ever since. Their leader, Calhanoglu, left Turkey at eighteen and built an empire in Germany and Italy. The thread that ties both nations together: home is not where you are born — it is where you fight.
