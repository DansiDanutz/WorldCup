And this one is true. In 2005, Ivory Coast reached their first World Cup, and their captain made a plea the world never forgot. They reached this World Cup with the meanest defence of all: only two defeats in eighteen games. At its heart is Moisés Caicedo, the destroyer who shields everything behind him, with Piero Hincapié and Willian Pacho, a wall built of stone. And up front, Enner Valencia — the man who simply scores at World Cups, again and again. The duel is Ivory Coast's flair against Ecuador's wall: the fastest wingers in the group against the calmest defenders in the world.
