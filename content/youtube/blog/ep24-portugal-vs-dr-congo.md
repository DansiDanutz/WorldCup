## The secret nobody talks about

West Germany, 1974. A nation then called Zaire became the first Black African team ever to reach a World Cup — African champions, the Leopards, kings of their continent. But the tournament turned cruel: nine-nil to Yugoslavia, and back home a dictator was watching, and seething. Mobutu had the squad threatened over how badly they lost; their bonuses were taken. These men were playing in fear. So against Brazil, Mwepu Ilunga charged from the wall and hammered the ball away — a yellow card, on purpose, a protest no one understood. It was never ignorance. It was courage. That is the shirt DR Congo wear tonight — not a punchline, but a door, kicked open for a whole continent.

## Portugal's golden chase

Across from them: Portugal. European champions in 2016, but a World Cup has always slipped through their fingers. Bruno Fernandes unlocks any door. Bernardo Silva is all silk and cunning. Rafael Leão flies down the left, and Rúben Dias is a wall in dark red. And leading them, one last time, Cristiano Ronaldo — forty-one years old, at his sixth World Cup, chasing the one trophy he has never lifted.

## The spirit of the Leopards

But the Leopards did not come to make up the numbers. Yoane Wissa is sharp and fearless. Cédric Bakambu has scored on almost every continent he has played. Chancel Mbemba marshals the back, a captain who has tamed strikers across Europe. Silas Katompa carries it forward, Arthur Masuaku gives them steel — a young, hungry team with an old, proud soul. So here is the duel: Portugal's golden chase against the spirit of the Leopards.
