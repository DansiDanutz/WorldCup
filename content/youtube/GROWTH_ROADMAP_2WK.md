## Decision gate (end of Week 2)
Per `CLAUDE.md` North Star: if consolidated, fully-pushed uploads won't reach
**3,000 watch-hours within ~60–90 days**, escalate (more premieres, longer/higher-
retention episodes, heavier organic distribution) or shift effort to the
**Shorts-views path**. Never keep doing what isn't moving watch hours.

## What we deliberately do NOT do (from the Romayroh analysis)
- No multi-channel niche-hopping / RPM arbitrage — we are ONE branded series tied
  to the worldcup26.world game. Consolidate, don't fragment.
- No generic "highlights" / "breaking news" packaging — keep the emotional
  story-brand identity (face + gold text + verified hook).
