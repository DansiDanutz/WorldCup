## Which path is realistic: watch hours, not Shorts views

- **3,000 watch hours / 365 days** = 180,000 watch-minutes. At a ~3-min average
  view on a 5-min episode, that's ~**60,000 full-equivalent views/year (~165/day)**.
  Achievable with a growing public catalog + premieres + playlist session time.
- **3,000,000 Shorts views / 90 days** = ~**33,000 Shorts views/day**. Far harder
  unless a Short goes viral.

➡️ **Primary target: the 3,000-hour path.** Treat Shorts as the *discovery engine
that feeds long-form watch time* (and subs), not as the monetization path itself.

## The watch-hours playbook (everything compounds here)

1. **Consolidate all 13 episodes onto `@DansLab-Kimi`, public.** Biggest single jump.
2. **Playlist "All Episodes" + auto-play + end screens → next episode.** Session
   watch time is the cheapest watch-hour multiplier we have.
3. **Premiere every new episode ≥48h before kickoff** (the hard rule) — premieres
   bank concentrated live watch time + notifications.
4. **Shorts 2/day** funnel viewers to full episodes (pinned comment + end CTA).
   Their job: convert a 30-sec view into a 5-min watch-hour.
5. **Retention edits** (cold-open hook, ChapterBar, "COMING UP — MINUTE 83"
   flash, speed-ramp climax) lift average view duration = more hours per view.
6. **Big-nation episodes** (France/Spain/Portugal/Argentina) — more views ×
   longer sessions. Fill the empty pipeline (`PREMIERE_CALENDAR.md`).
7. **Get-notified toggle ON** so the YPP application opens the day we cross 3,000h.

## What NOT to do
- ❌ Don't chase the 3M-Shorts-views path as primary — the math doesn't favour a small channel.
- ❌ Don't leave episodes on two channels "to be safe" — it splits the watch hours that unlock the money.
- ❌ Don't set/leave videos as Made for kids — it kills the very fan-funding features we're unlocking.
- ❌ Don't buy views — YPP review rejects inorganic watch time.
