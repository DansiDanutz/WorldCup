# Did You Know? Deep Dive — plan for the next 10 episodes

**Format:** 5-minute stories about **famous, searchable players** — the names people type
into YouTube — each built on one verified "did you know?" secret. Every visual is drawn
in code (typography, faceless shirts, maps, pitch diagrams, stats); **no Higgsfield, no
generated footage, no faces.** Brian narrates. Every episode adds one legend card to the
album that unlocks by watching it.

How to build one: `.claude/skills/dyk-deep-dive/SKILL.md`. Queue (machine-readable,
used by the daily run): `marketing/deep-dives/plan.json`.

## Why these ten

Search demand first: the 2026 champions (Yamal, Rodri), the final's other side (Messi),
the Golden Boot (Mbappé), the biggest global names (Ronaldo, Salah, Haaland, Kane,
Modrić, Bellingham). Each hook is a secret most fans don't know, verified before scripting.

## Title pattern

`<Player> + the secret, as a searchable sentence | Did You Know?` — e.g.
*Messi Bathed Lamine Yamal as a Baby — 19 Years Later They Met in the World Cup Final*.
Name first, ≤100 characters, honest (the video pays it off).

## Verification notes (from research, 13 Oct 2026)

- Haaland's long-jump record traces back to one 2020 claim — say **"reportedly"**.
- Ronaldo "named after Ronald Reagan" was **not** confirmed — leave it out.
- Kane: released by Arsenal at 8 (some reports say 9) — say "as a boy" or "at eight" with the Brady quote.
- Salah: ~10 hours a day of travel is well sourced; "4.5 hours each way" is single-source.
- Every episode re-verifies its own facts before scripting (skill LAW #2).
