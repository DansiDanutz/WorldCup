## His core philosophy (the 2026 thesis)
1. **Retention + quality is the whole game.** If automated videos keep people
   watching, the algorithm keeps promoting them — subs/age/history matter less.
   (Matches the other 2026 algo source in `ALGORITHM_2026_CONFIG_PLAN.md`.)
2. **Win the niche before it saturates.** Pick a **brand-new, low-competition**
   space; be one of the first in it. When it crowds, **pivot to an adjacent
   market** (Florida → Hawaii real estate; any city/region works globally).
3. **Make AI do the heavy lifting** — but feed it real research so content is
   fact-based and credible, not hallucinated.
4. **Chase RPM, not just views.** He targets high-RPM niches (US real estate /
   finance, RPM ~$9+) so modest view counts still earn well.

## His exact production system (faceless automation pipeline)
1. **Research:** Google the topic/news (e.g. "Hawaii real estate market crashing"),
   grab 2–3 credible articles, paste into a doc, export to PDF.
2. **Script:** Upload the PDFs to ChatGPT with a **reusable prompt** + the target
   title; AI writes a complete, accurate, retention-optimized script from the
   sourced facts (no fabrication).
3. **Voiceover:** **ElevenLabs** — pick a voice, paste the script in ~3-paragraph
   chunks, export. "~10 min" to natural narration.
4. **Edit:** AI/drag-and-drop editor for DIY, or **outsource on Fiverr/Upwork
   (~$20/video)**.
5. **Thumbnail:** **reusable templates** — "half-and-half" split, an
   "EXCLUSIVE / BREAKING NEWS" bar, a Google-sourced image, **one bold word**
   ("Crashing?"), tweaked colors/filters. Or buy at **~$5/thumbnail**.
6. **Publish + repeat**, then **duplicate the workflow into more channels** to scale.

## Packaging tricks (titles & thumbnails)
- **Curiosity + stakes in the title**, often a question ("…Crashing?") and a
  big concrete number/claim. His own teaching titles lean on income hooks and
  "nobody is doing this" framing.
- **Thumbnail = template + one bold word + a "breaking news" cue.** Consistency
  of template across the channel builds a recognizable brand and speeds production.
- **Title ↔ thumbnail are one promise**; the thumbnail raises the stakes, the
  title delivers specifics.

## Retention tactics
- High-fidelity AI voice (indistinguishable from human) to avoid trust drop-off.
- Script written for retention from viral data — strong open, no dead air.
- Quality audio + tight pacing → higher retention → more algorithmic push.

## Monetization & scaling

- **Scale by systemizing**: reusable script prompt + thumbnail templates +

- Community deliverables that reveal his system: 80+ training videos, **daily
  hand-picked niches**, a **full script-prompt library**, algorithm research,

**Already aligned (we do this):** faceless, AI visuals, **ElevenLabs (Brian) VO**,
research-then-write, retention-first 2026 thesis, packaging = title×thumbnail.
